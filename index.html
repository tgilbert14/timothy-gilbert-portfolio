    • Résumé

    Elevation: Mapzen Terrain Tiles on AWS (USGS 3DEP, SRTM), heights drawn at 2×. The weather, city lights, ground cover and sun path are drawn for effect, and the clock is read off that sun path.

    Trails, roads, rail and buildings: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Trail lengths are the mapped lengths. Building heights are tagged where known and estimated otherwise. The stadium and the cathedral towers are modelled by hand, not surveyed. Trees are scattered for effect, in parks, in yards and on the high ground, not mapped one by one. The A on Sentinel Peak is drawn about three times life size. The state line is drawn from its surveyed corner points.
